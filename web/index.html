FM-1 Transporter

1Connect

Plug in the Transporter, then pick its second serial port.

2Loader

Switch the FM-1 on and wait until the Transporter's LED stops blinking red, briefly shows other colours, then shows green alone (on the Debug Probe, the green LED by the UART port). Then choose wl82loader.bin (jl-uboot-tool). It stays in your browser and the Transporter's RAM. A black-screen unit: switch it off, press Reconnect, pick the port again, then switch it on.

3FM-1

Checks the chip key and flash ID. Stock V15 enters boot mode by itself.

4Backup

Reads the whole flash twice and saves it.

5Write

Choose a full flash image. Only changed sectors in 0x4000–0x92FFF are written and verified.
